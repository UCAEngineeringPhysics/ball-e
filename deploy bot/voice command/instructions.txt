You need to start the Flask application from your terminal. 
Make sure you are in the directory where your app.py lives.

~/vosk_realtime_transcription
source venv/bin/activate

Run the application:

python app.py

You should see terminal output saying the Vosk model loaded 
successfully and the server is starting on https://0.0.0.0:5000. 
Leave this terminal window open.

Grab your phone or open a web browser on your computer 
(it must be connected to the same Wi-Fi network as your Raspberry Pi).

Navigate to your Raspberry Pi's IP address on port 5000 using HTTPS. 
It will look something like this:
https://192.168.1.100:5000 (Replace with your Pi's actual IP address).

Accept the Security Warning: 
Because you generated your own SSL certificates, 
your browser will flag it as "Not Secure." 
Click Advanced and then Proceed to [IP address] 
(this is completely safe for local development).

When the page loads, your browser will ask for microphone permissions.
Click Allow


If your web interface has a Start or Record button, 
press it to open the WebSocket connection.

Speak your trigger phrase clearly into your device's microphone:
"start"

The web interface should transcribe your words on the screen as you speak.

As soon as the phrase is recognized and finalized on your screen, look at the terminal window where your app.py is running.

You should instantly see this message pop up:
[SYSTEM] Voice command recognized! Executing script...

Right after that, your Raspberry Pi will execute 
your terminal commands in the background—it will load your 
Hailo AI environment and launch odom_course.py.
